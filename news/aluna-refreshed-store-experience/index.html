Aluna Unveils a Refreshed Store Experience

A modern, immersive space designed to bring global brands closer to our customers in Kuwait.

SHARE

All stories

Aluna, part of the UTC family, has unveiled a refreshed store experience at its latest location, bringing together technology, lifestyle and global brands under one roof.

The new store design focuses on creating a modern and immersive environment that enhances the way customers explore, interact and experience the latest innovations in consumer electronics and lifestyle products.

Inside the refreshed Aluna store.

With an open layout, dedicated experience zones and a curated product selection, the refreshed store reflects Aluna’s commitment to offering a premium retail experience. Customers can now discover a wider range of global brands, receive personalised support and experience products in an environment designed for comfort and inspiration.

The refreshed store is another step forward in Aluna’s journey to bring the world’s leading brands closer to customers in Kuwait, while continuing to set new standards in retail excellence.
