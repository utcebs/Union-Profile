Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most — about partnering with UTC, our brands, retail stores and after-sales service. Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch and our team will be glad to help.

General
I have a new brand that I want to get into the region — why should I choose UTC as my distributor?
You should use UTC as your distributor as we have over 60 years of proven experience in the GCC region and have established ourselves as a premier distributor and retailer, offering both new and established brands across many different markets and categories. We already have over 100 world-recognised brands including Gillette, Oral-B, Braun, Duracell, Hanes, General Air Conditioning, Glem Gas, Vileda, Chantelle and Parker. We also have 17 retail outlets across Kuwait and we are looking to expand further. In addition, our Service Centres operate across Kuwait to address the needs of our customers. We have the contacts and supply-chain support to distribute any brand across the GCC region and increase your sales and profit. If you have a brand or product and would like to increase your market presence in the MENA region, please contact us.
I have just moved to Kuwait with my family — why should I buy from UTC retail outlets and buy your brands?
At UTC we offer the best brands at the most competitive prices. We have over 90% market penetration in the region, meaning you will see our products at 90% of retail outlets, including hypermarkets, grocery shops and convenience stores. If you see a brand distributed by UTC, you can be confident that it is a brand of high quality that you can trust. We have many years of experience bringing high-quality brands from around the world, and we can assure our customers that they are only purchasing the best available products.
I have just heard of UTC — what kind of products and brands do you have available?
At UTC we offer a wide variety of products across a large number of categories, including accessories, clothing, electrical appliances, perfumes & cosmetics, personal grooming, food and homeware. We have over 100 world-recognised brands including Gillette, Oral-B, Braun, Duracell, Hanes, General Air Conditioning, Glem Gas, Vileda, Chantelle and Parker. For more information please explore our divisions.
I have a new brand of product — can you help me get it into the MENA market?
Yes. We have the distribution channels and supply-chain infrastructure to distribute your product anywhere in Kuwait. We have been in this industry for over 60 years and represent over 100 established brands throughout the region — brands such as Gillette, Oral-B, Braun, Duracell, Hanes, General Air Conditioning, Glem Gas, Vileda, Chantelle and Parker are all distributed by UTC here in Kuwait. We have over 90% market penetration in Kuwait, so you can be sure that if you want your brand to reach a lucrative market in the GCC, UTC are the people to deal with. We operate 17 retail stores throughout Kuwait and also distribute to all major retail outlets and hypermarkets. We have a strong customer-service team and brand-management division to ensure your products get the exposure they need. For more information, please don't hesitate to contact our team to discover how our proven experience can lead to your brand's success.
Do you offer servicing and repairs for appliances, even if they are not your brands?
Yes we do. UTC operates and runs its own Service and Repair Centre in Kuwait, providing first-class after-sales care and ensuring our customers get many years of enjoyment from their purchase. We service upwards of 200,000 air conditioners alone per year — so when you have a problem, you know who to call. All appliances sold by UTC have the backup of our Service Centre. Our trained and experienced engineers are usually able to fix minor problems at the customer's home — this includes cookers, washing machines, dryers, refrigerators, water coolers, microwaves and air conditioners. When an issue cannot be rectified on site, our technicians will repair the appliance at our fully equipped Service Centre.
I need a new electrical appliance but can't pay the full price right now — do you offer credit?
Yes we do. Our credit programme offers you an easy payment plan to suit your budget — you can have the products you need now and leave the financing for a later date. Our fast and easy process lets you select the products, complete the credit application (subject to qualifying criteria) and arrange credit on any of our big-ticket items.
Do you have retail shops? Where can I buy your products?
Yes we do. UTC has its own retail stores around Kuwait, and we are able to stock your brands there. We have 17 stores and the number is growing. You can find all of our locations — including GPS coordinates — on our Our Stores page. Our stores are in premium locations around Kuwait; please contact us for more information about our retail store locations.
Do you offer any discounts or seasonal promotions? How can I find out about these?
From time to time UTC offers discounts and sales across our various retail outlets. You will see our advertisements in local publications and newspapers — however, the website is always the best place to check.
